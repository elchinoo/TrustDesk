# Training and Equipment Policy

## Training budget
Each employee has a training budget of 1,500 USD per calendar year. The manager approves training up to this budget. Training above the budget needs Director approval. Training must be related to the employee's current or planned role.

## Pre-approval
Courses and conferences with a total cost above 500 USD must be approved before registration through a training request. Approval after payment is not guaranteed.

## Certification exams
Exam fees are reimbursed after the employee passes. Retake fees are not reimbursed.

## Repayment
An employee who leaves Acme within 12 months after training that cost more than 1,000 USD repays the cost of that training.

## Equipment
Standard laptops are replaced every 36 months through IT. Peripherals up to 300 USD are ordered from the IT catalogue. Equipment above 1,000 USD must be bought through IT procurement and is never reimbursed as an expense.
