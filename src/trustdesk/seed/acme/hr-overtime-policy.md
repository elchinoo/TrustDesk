# Overtime Policy

## Who is eligible
Employees at levels L1 to L3 are non-exempt and are paid for overtime. Employees at level L4 and above are exempt and receive no overtime pay.

## Approval
Overtime must be approved by the manager before it is worked. In an emergency it may be approved within 24 hours afterwards. The request is submitted within 3 working days after the hours were worked.

## Limits
Overtime is limited to 10 hours per week and 40 hours per month. More than that needs Director approval.

## Compensation
Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5 times the hourly rate for overtime on working days and 2 times for work on weekends and public holidays. Exempt employees may receive time off in lieu at one hour for each approved overtime hour, up to 8 hours per month, agreed with the manager.

## Overtime meals
A meal allowance of up to 25 USD is reimbursed when approved overtime continues after 20:00.
