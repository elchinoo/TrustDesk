# Business Travel Policy

## Pre-approval
Every business trip needs approval before anything is booked. The direct manager approves trips with an estimated total cost of up to 3,000 USD. A Director approves trips between 3,000 and 10,000 USD. A VP approves trips above 10,000 USD and any trip to a destination on the Legal high-risk list. A trip request states the destination, purpose and estimated cost.

## Trip length
A business trip may last up to 14 days. Longer trips need Director approval. Personal days added to a trip are taken as vacation and any extra cost is paid by the employee.

## Flights
Flights are booked at least 14 days ahead where possible. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class needs VP approval.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is 200 USD per night. In high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich and Tokyo) the limit is 320 USD per night. Higher rates need Director approval before booking.

## Meals on trips
A meal allowance of 75 USD per day applies for domestic travel and 95 USD per day for international travel. Meals paid by a customer or event are deducted from the allowance. Receipts are not needed for the daily allowance but are needed for individual claims.

## Ground transport
Public transport is preferred. Taxis and ride-hailing are allowed for airport transfers and when public transport is not practical. Rental cars are compact class unless the manager approves otherwise. Mileage in a personal car is reimbursed at 0.67 USD per mile.

## After the trip
The employee submits expenses within 30 days of returning. Expenses for a trip that was not approved in advance need Director approval.
