# Incident Response Policy

## Detection and triage
Security events are collected into a central log platform and monitored 24x7 by the on-call security engineer. Events are classified Sev1 to Sev4 within 30 minutes of detection.

## Customer notification
Acme notifies affected customers of a confirmed personal data breach without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after confirmation. Notifications include scope, impact, and remediation steps.

## Post-incident review
A blameless post-incident review is held within 5 business days of a Sev1 or Sev2 incident. Findings and corrective actions are tracked to completion.

## Testing
The incident response plan is exercised with a tabletop exercise at least twice a year.
