# Parental, Bereavement and Unpaid Leave Policy

## Parental leave entitlement
Birth mothers receive 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. The second parent, including fathers and partners, receives 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Adoptive parents receive the same leave as birth parents of the same role. Parental leave must be started within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Parental leave notice and documents
The employee gives at least 30 calendar days of notice before the expected start date, except where the birth or adoption comes earlier than expected. HR needs a medical certificate of the expected date of birth, or the adoption placement papers. HR approves parental leave, not the manager.

## Bereavement leave
After the death of a spouse, partner, child, parent or sibling the employee receives 5 paid working days. After the death of a grandparent, parent-in-law or sibling-in-law the employee receives 3 paid working days. After the death of any other relative or close friend the employee receives 1 paid working day. No notice period applies; the manager is told as soon as possible.

## Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of 5 working days up to 90 calendar days may be requested after 12 months of service. It needs at least 30 calendar days of notice and the approval of both the manager and HR. Benefits that depend on salary are suspended during unpaid leave. Unpaid leave of more than 90 calendar days needs VP approval.
