# Customer Entertainment and Gifts Policy

## Meals and entertainment
Meals and entertainment with customers and partners are reimbursed up to 100 USD per person per event. Events with more than 8 attendees need Director approval. The claim must state the business purpose and list the attendees with their company. The receipt must be itemised.

## Pre-approval
Entertainment with a total cost above 500 USD must be approved by a Director before the event.

## Alcohol
Alcohol is only acceptable in moderation and may not be more than 25 percent of the bill.

## Gifts
A gift to a customer or partner may be worth at most 75 USD per person per calendar year. Cash and gift cards are never allowed. Gifts must be reported in the claim with the name of the recipient and their company.

## Public sector and anti-bribery
No gifts, meals or entertainment may be given to government officials or public sector employees without written approval from Legal. Hospitality must never be offered to influence a decision. During an open tender or procurement with a customer, no gifts or entertainment may be given to that customer. Any suspected bribery must be reported to Legal immediately.
