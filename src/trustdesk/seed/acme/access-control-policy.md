# Access Control Policy

## Authentication
Employees authenticate with single sign-on and phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication (MFA) using (FIDO2 security keys). Shared accounts are prohibited. Service accounts use short-lived credentials issued by the secrets manager.

## Least privilege
Access is granted by role and reviewed quarterly by system owners. Production access requires an approved ticket, is time-boxed to a maximum of 8 hours, and every session is recorded.

## Offboarding
Access for departing employees is revoked within 24 hours of their last day, and immediately for involuntary terminations. HR and IT reconcile the access list monthly.
