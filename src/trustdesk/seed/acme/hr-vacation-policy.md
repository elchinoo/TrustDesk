# Vacation and Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid vacation per calendar year. After 5 years of service the entitlement rises to 30 working days. Part-time employees receive a proportional amount. In the year of hire the entitlement is prorated by full months worked.

## Probation
Vacation may be taken after 90 days of employment. During the first 90 days vacation needs the written agreement of the manager and HR.

## Notice periods
A vacation of up to 5 working days needs at least 7 calendar days of notice. A vacation of 6 to 10 working days needs at least 14 calendar days of notice. A vacation of more than 10 working days needs at least 30 calendar days of notice. Requests with shorter notice may only be approved by the manager in exceptional circumstances and the reason must be recorded.

## Length and approval
The direct manager approves vacations of up to 10 working days. Vacations of 11 to 15 working days need Director approval. No employee may take more than 15 consecutive working days of vacation without VP approval.

## Team coverage
No more than 40 percent of a team may be on approved leave on the same working days. The manager checks coverage before approving. Customer Support must keep at least two people on duty every working day.

## Blackout periods
Employees in the Finance department may not take vacation during the last 5 working days of each fiscal quarter or the first 5 working days of the fiscal year. Exceptions need the Finance Director.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Remaining unused days are forfeited unless local law says otherwise. Vacation days are never paid out except on termination, as required by law.

## Retroactive requests
Vacation cannot be requested after it has started. In a genuine emergency HR may approve a retroactive request. Sick leave that interrupts a vacation, supported by a medical certificate, may be converted back into vacation days.
