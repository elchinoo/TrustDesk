# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

## Customer data
Customer data is retained for the duration of the contract. Within 30 days of termination, customer data is deleted from production systems and within 90 days from backups. Customers may request deletion at any time through support.

## Logs
Security and audit logs are retained for 13 months. Application debug logs are retained for 30 days and must not contain personal data.

## Sub-processors
Acme maintains a public list of sub-processors and gives customers 30 days notice before adding a new one.
