# Remote Work Policy

## Default arrangement
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without a request, as long as their team keeps agreed coverage. No request is needed for these days.

## Fully remote weeks
A request is needed for 5 or more consecutive remote working days. The manager may approve up to 4 fully remote weeks per calendar year.

## Working from another country
An employee may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. The request must be made at least 30 calendar days in advance and needs approval from the manager and from Legal and Tax. Work from abroad is only allowed in countries where Acme has a legal entity, unless Legal and Tax approve in writing. It is never allowed in countries on the Legal sanctions list. Longer stays or relocation must go through HR.

## Security while remote
Employees use the company laptop, the company VPN and full disk encryption. Public Wi-Fi is only allowed through the VPN. Customer data must not be stored on personal devices.
