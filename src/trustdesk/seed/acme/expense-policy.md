# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## What is reimbursable
Acme reimburses reasonable expenses that have a business purpose and follow this policy. Expenses are reimbursed in the currency they were paid in, converted at the exchange rate on the expense date.

## Deadline
Expenses must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date. Older expenses are only reimbursed with an exception from the VP Finance.

## Receipts
A receipt or itemised invoice is required for every expense of 25 USD or more. A card slip alone is not enough. An expense below 25 USD may be claimed without a receipt if the employee explains it. For a missing receipt of 25 USD or more, a missing-receipt declaration approved by a Director is required, and an employee may use it at most 2 times per year.

## Approval thresholds
The direct manager approves expenses up to 500 USD. A Director approves expenses above 500 and up to 2,500 USD. The VP Finance approves expenses above 2,500 USD and up to 10,000 USD. The CFO approves anything above 10,000 USD. Nobody approves their own expense.

## Not reimbursable
Personal items, fines and traffic tickets, spouse or family travel, in-flight entertainment, minibar, gym fees, and laundry on trips shorter than 5 nights are not reimbursable. Alcohol is only reimbursable as part of a customer meal and may not exceed 25 percent of the bill.

## Duplicates
The same expense must not be claimed twice, for example on a card statement and as a cash claim. Duplicate claims are rejected and may lead to disciplinary action.

## Meals without travel
Team meals are reimbursed up to 30 USD per person with a list of attendees.

## Home office
A one-time home office setup allowance of 500 USD is available to each employee and may be claimed once every 24 months, with receipts. A monthly allowance of 40 USD covers internet and phone and needs a copy of the bill.

## Software and equipment
Purchases below 200 USD may be claimed with manager approval. Purchases from 200 up to 1,000 USD need prior written approval from the manager. Purchases above 1,000 USD must go through IT procurement and are not reimbursed.
