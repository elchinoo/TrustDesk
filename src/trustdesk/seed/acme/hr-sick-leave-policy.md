# Sick Leave Policy

## Paid sick leave
Employees receive up to 10 working days of fully paid sick leave per calendar year. Further sick leave in the same year is paid at 60 percent of salary for up to 60 additional working days and needs HR approval.

## Notification
The employee tells their manager before the start of the working day, and by 10:00 local time at the latest, on the first day of absence. Messaging the manager is enough.

## Medical certificate
A medical certificate is required for any sick leave of more than 3 consecutive working days. For absences of more than 10 consecutive working days HR contacts the employee to plan the return to work. A certificate is not needed for 3 days or fewer.

## Submitting the request
Sick leave may be submitted after it has started, up to 5 working days after the employee returns. A sick leave request made later than that needs HR approval and a written explanation.

## Overlap
Sick leave cannot overlap another approved leave. If an employee becomes ill during an approved vacation and provides a medical certificate, the sick days are recorded as sick leave and the vacation days are restored.

## Medical expenses
Acme does not reimburse personal medical costs. The only exceptions are medical costs caused by an injury at work, vaccinations required for work travel, and one eye examination per year for employees who use a screen daily, up to 150 USD per year with a receipt.
