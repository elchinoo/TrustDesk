# Approval Authority and Process

## Principles
Every request is decided by a person with the right authority. Nobody may approve their own request or a request from someone they are in a relationship with. An AI pre-validation may be used to prepare a decision but it never replaces the approver, who stays accountable for the decision.

## Leave
The direct manager approves vacation of up to 10 working days and informs HR. A Director approves vacation of more than 10 working days. HR approves parental leave, unpaid leave and sick leave beyond 10 working days. Sick leave of up to 10 working days is recorded by the manager and needs no approval.

## Travel and expenses
Travel is approved by the manager, Director or VP according to the estimated cost in the travel policy. Expenses are approved by the manager, Director, VP Finance or CFO according to the thresholds in the expense policy.

## Decisions and records
An approver records a reason when rejecting a request or asking the employee for more information, so the employee can see why. Decisions are kept for 7 years. A decision that goes against the compliance pre-check must include a written justification.

## Exceptions
Any exception to a policy needs the approval of the owner of that policy: HR for leave, Finance for expenses and Legal for gifts, hospitality, sanctions and work abroad. An exception is recorded with the person who granted it.
